# Scheduled Jobs in Quillo: Houseplants That Ask for Water

Plant Pal: houseplants that ask for water. A job on a schedule finds the thirsty ones and leaves a note, pages follow the plants live, and a clock on the page counts down to the next check with every.

Written for Quillo 0.8.6. `run.sh` downloads that Quillo the first time:

```sh
./run.sh --watch
```

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8080.
