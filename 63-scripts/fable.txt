The Snail Who Wanted to Fly

There was once a snail who lived under a cabbage leaf, and every morning the snail watched the swallows fly over the garden.
"I want to fly," said the snail to the cabbage.
The cabbage said nothing, because it was a cabbage.
So the snail asked the swallows. "How do you fly?"
"You just fly," said the swallows, and they flew away.
The snail climbed the garden wall. It took all day, and the wall was very tall, and the snail was very slow.
At the top, the snail looked down at the garden: the cabbage, the beans, the pond, the old cat asleep in the sun.
"Oh," said the snail. "So this is what the swallows see."
The wind came, and the snail held on to the wall, and the snail did not fly.
But the snail did not mind. From the top of the wall, the garden was the most beautiful thing the snail had ever seen.
The next morning the swallows flew over the garden, and the snail was on the wall, watching them, and the snail waved.
The swallows waved back.
