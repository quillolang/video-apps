# Fluffy, at last

After eleven flat pancakes, I finally made **fluffy** ones. The secret? Patience.

## What you need

- 1 cup of flour
- 1 cup of buttermilk
- 1 egg
- 2 spoons of sugar, and a pinch of salt
- 1 spoon of baking powder

## How

1. Mix the dry things, then the wet things.
2. Stir them together, *lumps and all*.
3. Let the batter rest for ten minutes. Don't skip this!
4. Cook on a medium pan until bubbles pop on top, then flip.

> A lumpy batter makes a light pancake. — my grandma
